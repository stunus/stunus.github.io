Privacy Policy - Clipsbyrade Uploader

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Clipsbyrade Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by a single creator to publish short gaming clips to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives: an access token and refresh token for that account; the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope; permission to upload and publish videos to that account through the video.upload and video.publish scopes; and the publishing status of the videos the app uploads, so it can show the link to the published post.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload video clips the account owner has approved to the account owner's own TikTok account, and to show the account owner whether a post was published and the link to it. The app does not read, collect or analyse other users' data, followers, messages or comments.

Storage

Tokens and settings are stored locally on the owner's own computer in a private configuration file. They are never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the TikTok account is connected. The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok (Settings, Security, Apps and services). Disconnecting, or deleting the local configuration file, removes all data the app holds.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app owner through the developer contact email registered for this app on TikTok for Developers.
