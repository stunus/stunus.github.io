Clipsbyrade Uploader

Clipsbyrade Uploader is a private desktop tool used by one creator to publish short gaming clips to their own TikTok account.

The creator reviews and approves each clip in a local dashboard, where the caption is shown and can be edited. Approved clips are published at a scheduled time to the creator's own account through the TikTok Content Posting API. The tool is not offered to the public and never posts to other accounts.
